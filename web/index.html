Paste the CSV from your OpenPowerlifting lifter page, or write one meet per line: the date, then 3 squat, 3 bench and 3 deadlift attempts, with a minus sign for a miss and 0 for an attempt not taken.         · how steeply make chances drop, day-to-day form
        Day form

        The day max varies around the expected max for two reasons: strength moves from day to day, and the expected max itself may be off. Lower the uncertainty if the max comes from a recent test or meet. The Lifter choice sets these and the make-rate numbers to those fitted to that group's meets, keeping any you changed.

        Make rate

        The chance of making an attempt falls along a logistic curve as the load nears the day's max. The midpoint is the load ÷ day max ratio with a 50% make rate. A technical miss, such as a depth call or a missed command, can happen at any load.
