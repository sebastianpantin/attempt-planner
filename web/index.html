    Not sure? Estimate them from your past meets

    Estimates your expected maxes today from the attempts you took, including the progress lifters typically make since their last meet, and shows your best made lifts next to them. Paste the CSV from your OpenPowerlifting lifter page, or write one meet per line: the date, then 3 squat, 3 bench and 3 deadlift attempts, with a minus sign for a miss and 0 for an attempt not taken. Only raw meets count, and nothing leaves your browser.

    For example: 2025-03-15 180 190 -197.5 120 125 -130 220 235 245

    Lifters gain less from one meet to the next the more meets they have done. If you only pasted your latest meets, say how many you did before them. Left empty, these are taken to be all your meets.

      Day form
      What is day form?

      The day max varies around the expected max for two reasons: strength moves from day to day, and the expected max itself may be off. Lower the uncertainty if the max comes from a recent test or meet. The "Make rates based on" choice sets these and the make-rate numbers to those fitted to that group's meets, keeping any you changed.

        Make rate

        The chance of making an attempt falls along a logistic curve as the load nears the day's max. The midpoint is the load ÷ day max ratio with a 50% make rate. A technical miss, such as a depth call or a missed command, can happen at any load.

            How it works

            The planner simulates 20,000 meets with your plan. In each one your max on the day is drawn around your expected max, since strength varies from day to day. Each attempt is then made or missed: the closer the weight is to that day's max, the lower the chance of making it, and any attempt can be a technical miss.

            Your plan sets the opener and how much to add after a make or a miss. The results count how often you reach your goal, the totals you are likely to post, and how often you bomb out by missing all three attempts on a lift.

            Find my best plan searches for the attempts with the best chance of reaching your goal, or the highest average total without a goal, while keeping the bomb-out risk within your limit.

            The make rates are fitted to raw meets in OpenPowerlifting. The model assumes raw lifting in kg, with 2.5 kg plate steps. The model in detail
