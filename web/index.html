Attempt planner

Plan openers and jumps for a powerlifting meet and see how the plan plays out over thousands of simulated meet days. Each lift's 2nd and 3rd attempts depend on whether the attempt before was made.

Meet

Sets the make-rate and day-form numbers under Advanced to those fitted to that group's meets, keeping any you changed.

    Past meets (optional)

    Estimates your expected maxes from the attempts you took. Paste the CSV from your OpenPowerlifting lifter page, or write one meet per line: the date, then 3 squat, 3 bench and 3 deadlift attempts, with a minus sign for a miss and 0 for an attempt not taken. Only raw meets count, and nothing leaves your browser.

    For example: 2025-03-15 180 190 -197.5 120 125 -130 220 235 245

      Advanced
      Day form

      The day max varies around the expected max for two reasons: strength moves from day to day, and the expected max itself may be off. Lower the uncertainty if the max comes from a recent test or meet.

      Make rate

      The chance of making an attempt falls along a logistic curve as the load nears the day's max. The midpoint is the load ÷ day max ratio with a 50% make rate. A technical miss, such as a depth call or a missed command, can happen at any load.
